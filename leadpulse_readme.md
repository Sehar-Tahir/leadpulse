
# ⚡ LeadPulse B2B Intelligence Engine

## Automated Local Lead Generation & Website Opportunity Finder for Pakistan

---

## 🌟 Overview

**LeadPulse** is a high-performance B2B lead generation and conversion engine built specifically for web developers, agencies, and CRM specialists (such as GoHighLevel experts) to target local Pakistani businesses such as salons, real estate agencies, cafes, clinics, and gyms that lack an official website.

The tool directly scrapes and verifies Google Maps directory profiles in real-time, filters out businesses with custom domains, scores them based on their review volume and ratings, auto-generates hyper-personalized WhatsApp outreach pitches, and allows instant export into professionally formatted Excel (`.xlsx`) files with a lifetime Master Vault history tracking system.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **🛡️ Strict No-Website Verification:** Automatically crawls Google Maps listings and filters out businesses with custom websites, flagging only website-free prospects (with optional support for flagging businesses using only social media links).
* **🧠 Hyper-Personalized Niche Outreach:** Automatically generates category-specific WhatsApp pitch links with customized value propositions tailored to the exact industry (e.g., property portals for Real Estate, digital menus for Restaurants, booking systems for Salons).
* **🗄️ Master Vault & Anti-Duplicate System:** Automatically saves extracted leads to a persistent CSV vault (`leadpulse_vault.csv`) so that previously scraped leads are never duplicated. Users can also select and delete specific search batches to free them back up for future extraction.
* **📥 Styled Excel Export:** Generates professionally formatted Excel reports in-memory complete with dark obsidian header styles, gold accent formatting, and auto-adjusted column widths.
* **💎 Obsidian & Champagne Gold UI:** Designed with a clean, high-end dark SaaS aesthetic powered by Streamlit and modern typography.

---

## 🚀 Tech Stack

* **Frontend & Dashboard:** Streamlit (Python)
* **Web Scraping Engine:** Playwright (Headless Chromium)
* **Data Processing & Export:** Pandas & OpenPyXL
* **Styling:** Custom CSS with Google Fonts (*Plus Jakarta Sans* & *Inter*)
